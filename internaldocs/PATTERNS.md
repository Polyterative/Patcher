- [patterns/RXJS_GOTCHAS.md](./patterns/RXJS_GOTCHAS.md) — small reactive edge cases worth remembering
- [testing/UNIT_TESTING.md](./testing/UNIT_TESTING.md) — shared unit test conventions and examples
